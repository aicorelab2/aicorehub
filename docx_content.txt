PROJECT 01  —  AIBER  |  Pharmaceutical Intelligence Database

⭐ SITUATION
The organization needed to build a structured intelligence database for pharmaceutical and drug companies by extracting relevant business information directly from their websites. These websites contained thousands of pages with massive, unstructured, and noisy content — making manual data collection completely infeasible at scale.

📋 TASK
Design and deliver an end-to-end automated data pipeline — from web scraping through intelligent categorization — that could extract, clean, filter, and categorize web content accurately according to business requirements, with minimal manual involvement.

Key technical decisions:
Explored and benchmarked Playwright, Selenium, BeautifulSoup, and Scrapy — combined 2–3 methodologies to maximize extraction accuracy
Implemented NLP-based filtering layer to reduce irrelevant web pages from thousands of links per website
Integrated RAG architecture to prevent LLM hallucinations and manage context window limitations
Benchmarked and tested multiple embedding models varying in dimensions and token capacities
Experimented with 10–15 different chunking strategies to optimize RAG performance

🏆  Reduced manual processing by 60–70% · Optimized RAG pipeline with best-fit chunking & embedding model · Built scalable pharma intelligence database
